Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on by and between ("Disclosing Party"), and ASKC DIGITAL WEB ("Receiving Party"), collectively referred to as the "Parties".

1. Scope of Confidential Information: The term "Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to, proprietary software ideas, technical algorithms, marketing strategies, business plans, financial records, client databases, and any UI/UX structures disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party prior to or during the project engagement.
2. Obligations of the Receiving Party: The Receiving Party absolutely agrees to: (a) hold the Confidential Information in the strictest confidence; (b) not disclose it to any third-party entity without prior written corporate authorization; and (c) use the Confidential Information exclusively for the purpose of evaluating or executing web development services for the Disclosing Party.
3. Exclusions from Confidentiality: The Receiving Party's obligations shall not realistically apply to information that: (a) is or inherently becomes part of the public domain through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was rightfully in their physical possession prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed without any usage of the Disclosing Party’s information; or (d) is legally mandated by court order.
4. Return or Destruction of Materials: Upon the immediate written request of the Disclosing Party, or termination of active services, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or entirely destroy all documentation, storage devices, and technical blueprints containing Confidential Information.
5. Survivability & Governing Law: The non-disclosure obligations explicitly dictated in this Agreement shall universally survive indefinitely beyond the termination of any resulting service contracts. This Agreement shall forcibly be governed in accordance with local jurisdictional corporate legislations.
